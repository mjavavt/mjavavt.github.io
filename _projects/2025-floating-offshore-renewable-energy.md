This project evaluates aero-hydrodynamic loading and structural response of floating offshore wind platforms under coupled environmental loads. My work connects marine dynamics, structural response, and design considerations for offshore renewable-energy systems.

I also examine technical resources, development constraints, and community-engagement considerations for future offshore wind in the Great Lakes.
