Designed course materials and hands-on experiments for middle-school students through Virginia Tech Imagination Summer Camps in 2021 and 2022. I also contributed demonstrations for the 2021 summer program while a graduate researcher.

These activities reflect my commitment to broadening participation in engineering and making marine and fluid-dynamics concepts accessible to early learners.
